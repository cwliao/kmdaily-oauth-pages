Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

KMDaily ("the app") is a personal automation tool built, operated, and used solely by its owner. It is not distributed to or used by the general public. This policy explains how the app handles data when it connects to the owner's own Google Account.

What data is accessed

The app requests the following Google OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.drafts.readonly — read-only access to Gmail drafts, used to let the app review and organize the owner's own draft emails as part of a personal knowledge-management workflow.

How the data is used

Data retrieved through this scope is processed locally by the owner's own tooling and is used only to support the owner's personal organization and automation tasks. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising or any purpose unrelated to the app's stated function.

Data storage and retention

Any data cached or stored by the app is kept only on infrastructure controlled by the owner and is retained only as long as needed for the app's personal use case. The owner may delete this data at any time.

Data sharing

No data obtained through this app is shared with any third party or external service beyond what is strictly necessary for the app to function on the owner's own behalf.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in how the app works. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cwliao@gmail.com.
