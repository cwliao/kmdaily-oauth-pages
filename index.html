KMDaily

A personal automation tool

KMDaily is a personal, single-user automation tool built and operated by its owner for private knowledge-management and productivity tasks (such as organizing personal notes and email drafts). It is not a public product or service, and it is not offered to the general public.

KMDaily connects to the owner's own Google Account using OAuth so it can read content the owner has explicitly authorized (for example, the owner's own Gmail drafts) in order to perform automated personal organization tasks.

For details on what data is accessed and how it is handled, see the Privacy Policy. Usage terms are described in the Terms of Service.
